## Focus

This cycle focuses on trust and first-use momentum.

- reduce high-frequency crash pain
- make the path to first success clearer
- remove a concrete interaction slowdown that active teams feel every day

## Why This Cycle

Recent support patterns show a split problem: new users struggle to get started cleanly, while existing teams still feel too much instability in certain moments. This cycle aims to improve both confidence in the product and confidence in the direction.
