## What We Are Focusing On

This cycle we are focusing on three bets:

- [[reduce-crash-reports]]
- [[improve-onboarding]]
- [[speed-up-viewport-selection]]

Together they address trust, first-use momentum, and day-to-day interaction quality.

## Why These Priorities

- crash reduction matters because reliability is a trust problem before it is a metrics problem
- onboarding matters because the first successful experience still takes too much work
- viewport selection matters because it is a contained performance bet with immediate user payoff

We are deliberately not trying to solve every pain point this cycle. We are choosing a small number of bets that the team can understand clearly and execute with autonomy.

## Likely Staffing Direction

- [[devon]] with [[emma]] on crash investigation and repro coverage
- [[claire]] with [[ben]] on onboarding flow improvements
- [[felix]] with [[alice]] on viewport selection responsiveness

## Risks And Tradeoffs

- crash reduction may uncover deeper instability than the appetite suggests
- onboarding work can drift into licensing questions if boundaries are not protected
- internal leverage work like [[improve-build-times]] is valuable, but remains out of cycle for now to keep the focus clear

## Open Questions

- how much of onboarding friction is product flow versus activation friction?
- do we need a follow-on reliability bet after crash reduction lands?
