This vault is set up to test an Obsidian-first priorities workflow.

## What this vault is modeling

- Shape Up style narrative notes for priorities
- lightweight scoring with RICE, ICE, and delay vs effort
- simple status tracking with `backlog`, `todo`, `working`, `cancelled`, and `done`
- cycle planning and team-facing brief writing
- people notes to help reason about staffing and team fit

## First setup inside Obsidian

- enable the core `Templates` feature and point it at `90 Templates/`
- enable `Bases`
- create the saved Bases described in [[Bases Setup]]

## What to pay attention to while testing

- whether creating and shaping priorities feels fast
- whether Bases gives enough structure for comparing candidates
- whether the notes stay readable outside of Obsidian
- whether staffing logic feels understandable with people notes and tags
- whether publishing a cycle brief feels light enough to maintain
