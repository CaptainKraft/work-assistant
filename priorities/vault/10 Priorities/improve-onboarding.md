## Problem

New users still have to work too hard to reach a first meaningful result. The current path asks them to make several small decisions before they have enough context to make them well.

## Why Now

This is one of the clearest gaps between product quality and product experience. Support effort is paying the price, and it weakens the team's story about why the product is worth learning.

## Desired Outcome

Reduce the time and confusion between launch and first successful result. A new user should understand the next step, see progress quickly, and feel guided rather than tested.

## Boundaries

- focus on the first-run path, starter content, and in-product guidance
- do not redesign licensing or account policy in this bet
- keep changes small enough to validate within one cycle

## Risks

- we may improve presentation without fixing the true activation bottleneck
- sample content could become a hidden maintenance burden
- [ ] confirm where users currently abandon the flow

## Staffing Notes

[[claire]] is the best fit for the experience design and messaging side. [[ben]] is a strong partner anywhere installer or setup flow behavior crosses into the bet.

## Decision Rationale

This is a strong current-cycle bet because it is strategically aligned, visible to users quickly, and small enough to keep bounded if we resist the pull toward platform-wide activation work.
