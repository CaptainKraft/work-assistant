## Problem

Activation behavior still feels inconsistent across products and surfaces. Users run into avoidable confusion about what is expected of them and when.

## Why Now

The opportunity is strategically important because activation is part of first trust, but the real edge cases still need mapping before this becomes a safe bet.

## Desired Outcome

Create one clearer activation story that reduces confusion without forcing a deeper billing or entitlement rewrite.

## Boundaries

- focus on flow consistency and clarity
- avoid rewriting policy, billing, or entitlement systems
- use this bet to simplify language and pathing, not to solve every activation exception

## Risks

- hidden platform edge cases could quickly expand the scope
- the real bottleneck may be policy complexity rather than UI flow
- [ ] list known exceptions before moving this to `todo`

## Staffing Notes

[[grace]] is the strongest owner because she understands the platform side. [[claire]] is the right partner to keep the work grounded in actual user understanding.

## Decision Rationale

This stays in backlog because the strategic value is high but the confidence is still low. It should only move forward once the edge cases are clear enough to preserve a real boundary.
